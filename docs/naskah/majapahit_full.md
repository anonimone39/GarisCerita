# Majapahit versi penuh, 16:9, narasi Indonesia dan Inggris

| # | Inggris | Indonesia |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Imagine a kingdom that once ruled the whole archipelago. | Bayangkan sebuah kerajaan yang pernah menguasai hampir seluruh Nusantara. |
| 2 | A kingdom that drove back the strongest army in the world. | Kerajaan yang berhasil memukul mundur pasukan terkuat di dunia. |
| 3 | Yet the same kingdom fell, not to an enemy from outside, but to a fight inside its own house. | Namun kerajaan yang sama juga runtuh, bukan karena musuh dari luar, tapi karena rebutan di dalam rumah sendiri. |
| 4 | This is the story of Majapahit, from the fire that gave it birth, to the wound that brought it down. | Inilah kisah Majapahit, dari api yang melahirkannya, sampai luka yang meruntuhkannya. |
| 5 | Chapter one. Fire over Singhasari. | Bab satu. Api di Singhasari. |
| 6 | The year is 1292. Singhasari is at the height of its power, under King Kertanegara. | Tahun 1292. Singhasari sedang di puncak kekuasaan, di bawah Raja Kertanegara. |
| 7 | He refused Kublai Khan, ruler of the Mongols, and even humiliated his envoy. | Ia berani menolak tunduk pada Kubilai Khan, penguasa Mongol, bahkan mempermalukan utusannya. |
| 8 | But in Kediri, a ruler nursed an old grudge. His name was Jayakatwang. | Tapi di Kediri, ada seorang penguasa yang menyimpan dendam lama. Namanya Jayakatwang. |
| 9 | When Singhasari let its guard down, Jayakatwang struck. Kertanegara was killed, and the palace burned. | Saat Singhasari lengah, Jayakatwang menyerang. Kertanegara tewas, dan istana pun terbakar. |
| 10 | In the chaos, the king's son-in-law, Raden Wijaya, escaped with his followers. | Di tengah kekacauan, menantu sang raja, Raden Wijaya, berhasil kabur bersama para pengikutnya. |
| 11 | He fled to Madura, and asked Arya Wiraraja for protection. | Ia lari ke Madura, meminta perlindungan pada Arya Wiraraja. |
| 12 | With Arya Wiraraja's help, Raden Wijaya pretended to surrender to Jayakatwang. | Dengan bantuan Arya Wiraraja, Raden Wijaya pura-pura menyerah dan tunduk pada Jayakatwang. |
| 13 | Jayakatwang believed him, and gave him the forest of Tarik. | Jayakatwang percaya, lalu memberinya sebidang hutan bernama Tarik. |
| 14 | But it was all an act. | Padahal, itu cuma akting. |
| 15 | There, Raden Wijaya quietly built a base. His followers cut down trees, and ate the fruit of the maja, which tasted bitter. | Di sana, Raden Wijaya diam-diam membangun markas. Para pengikutnya menebang pohon, dan memakan buah maja yang rasanya pahit. |
| 16 | That, as the story goes, is how Majapahit got its name. | Dari situlah, konon, nama Majapahit lahir. |
| 17 | Meanwhile, across the sea, Kublai Khan had not forgotten the insult to his envoy. | Sementara itu, di seberang lautan, Kubilai Khan belum lupa penghinaan terhadap utusannya. |
| 18 | According to Chinese records, he sent some twenty to thirty thousand troops, with hundreds of ships, to punish Kertanegara. | Menurut catatan Tiongkok, ia mengirim sekitar dua puluh sampai tiga puluh ribu pasukan, dengan ratusan kapal, untuk menghukum Kertanegara. |
| 19 | But the moment they landed on Java, they heard confusing news. Kertanegara was already dead, and Jayakatwang was in power. | Namun begitu mendarat di Jawa, mereka mendapat kabar yang membingungkan. Kertanegara sudah tewas, dan Jayakatwang yang berkuasa. |
| 20 | The Mongol commanders lost their bearings. Who was the real enemy? | Komandan Mongol kehilangan arah. Siapa musuh yang sebenarnya? |
| 21 | That was when Raden Wijaya saw a golden opportunity. | Di saat itulah, Raden Wijaya melihat peluang emas. |
| 22 | He declared his loyalty to Kublai Khan, and offered to work together. | Ia menyatakan tunduk pada Kubilai Khan, dan menawarkan kerja sama. |
| 23 | Your enemy is in Kediri. Let's attack together! | Musuh kalian ada di Kediri. Ayo kita serang bareng! |
| 24 | In April 1293, the allied army stormed Daha, the capital of Kediri. | April 1293, pasukan gabungan menyerbu Daha, ibu kota Kediri. |
| 25 | The battle lasted from morning until afternoon. Kediri fell, and Jayakatwang surrendered. | Pertempuran berlangsung dari pagi sampai siang. Kediri hancur, dan Jayakatwang menyerah. |
| 26 | The Mongol commanders thought their mission was done. | Para komandan Mongol mengira tugas mereka sudah selesai. |
| 27 | But this was the turning point. | Tapi inilah titik baliknya. |
| 28 | Raden Wijaya asked to go home to prepare tribute, and two princesses as gifts for the Emperor. | Raden Wijaya minta izin pulang ke Majapahit, dengan alasan menyiapkan upeti dan dua putri persembahan untuk sang Kaisar. |
| 29 | He asked for unarmed escorts, because, he said, the princesses feared weapons. | Ia meminta Mongol mengirim pengawal tanpa senjata, karena katanya para putri takut melihat senjata. |
| 30 | On the road, that party was ambushed. Then the main Mongol camp was suddenly attacked. | Di tengah jalan, rombongan itu disergap. Lalu markas utama Mongol diserang mendadak. |
| 31 | More than three thousand Mongols were killed, and the rest fled to their ships. | Lebih dari tiga ribu prajurit Mongol tewas, dan sisanya terpaksa kabur kembali ke kapal. |
| 32 | In late May 1293, the strongest army in the world sailed home to China. | Akhir Mei 1293, pasukan terkuat di dunia itu angkat sauh dan pulang ke Tiongkok. |
| 33 | Months later, around November 1293, Raden Wijaya was crowned the first king of Majapahit, as Kertarajasa Jayawardhana. | Beberapa bulan kemudian, sekitar November 1293, Raden Wijaya dinobatkan sebagai raja pertama Majapahit, bergelar Kertarajasa Jayawardhana. |
| 34 | This war was not won by the biggest army, but by the smartest strategy. | Perang ini tidak dimenangkan oleh pasukan terbesar, tapi oleh strategi paling cerdas. |
| 35 | Chapter two. A wedding that became a tragedy. | Bab dua. Pesta yang berubah jadi tragedi. |
| 36 | Sixty-four years later, Majapahit is at its peak, under King Hayam Wuruk and Prime Minister Gajah Mada. | Enam puluh empat tahun kemudian, Majapahit berada di puncak kejayaan, di bawah Raja Hayam Wuruk dan Mahapatih Gajah Mada. |
| 37 | Gajah Mada once swore the Palapa Oath. He would not enjoy palapa until the archipelago was united under Majapahit. | Gajah Mada pernah bersumpah, Sumpah Palapa. Ia tidak akan menikmati palapa, sebelum Nusantara bersatu di bawah Majapahit. |
| 38 | One kingdom still had not bowed. Sunda. | Satu kerajaan belum juga tunduk. Sunda. |
| 39 | In 1357, Hayam Wuruk fell in love with Dyah Pitaloka, the princess of Sunda, and proposed. | Tahun 1357, Hayam Wuruk jatuh hati pada Dyah Pitaloka, putri Raja Sunda, lalu melamarnya. |
| 40 | King Linggabuana agreed. He sailed to Majapahit with his family and a small party, fewer than a hundred people. | Raja Linggabuana setuju. Ia berlayar ke Majapahit bersama keluarga dan rombongan kecil, kurang dari seratus orang. |
| 41 | They did not bring an army, because they came for a wedding. | Bukan membawa pasukan perang, karena mereka datang untuk hajatan. |
| 42 | They camped at Bubat Field near the capital, waiting for the wedding day. | Mereka berkemah di Lapangan Bubat, tak jauh dari ibu kota Majapahit, menunggu hari pernikahan. |
| 43 | But Gajah Mada had other plans. | Tapi Gajah Mada punya rencana lain. |
| 44 | He rejected an equal marriage. Dyah Pitaloka must be handed over as an offering, a sign that Sunda had submitted. | Ia menolak pernikahan yang setara. Dyah Pitaloka harus diserahkan sebagai persembahan, tanda bahwa Sunda takluk. |
| 45 | King Linggabuana was furious. The wedding was cancelled, and he chose to go home with his head held high. | Raja Linggabuana murka. Pernikahan dibatalkan, dan ia memilih pulang dengan kepala tegak. |
| 46 | But Majapahit troops had surrounded Bubat. | Tapi pasukan Majapahit sudah mengepung Bubat. |
| 47 | The Sundanese were hopelessly outnumbered, dozens against thousands. | Rombongan Sunda kalah jumlah telak. Puluhan orang melawan ribuan prajurit. |
| 48 | Yet they chose to fight to the end. King Linggabuana fell. | Namun mereka memilih bertarung sampai akhir. Raja Linggabuana gugur. |
| 49 | According to tradition, Dyah Pitaloka took her own life to protect her honor. | Menurut tradisi, Dyah Pitaloka pun mengakhiri hidupnya demi menjaga kehormatan. |
| 50 | Hayam Wuruk is said to have grieved, and regretted it. | Hayam Wuruk dikisahkan berduka, dan menyesal. |
| 51 | Gajah Mada lost the court's trust, and was retired to Madakaripura. | Gajah Mada kehilangan kepercayaan istana, dan dipensiunkan ke Madakaripura. |
| 52 | Majapahit and Sunda stayed estranged for centuries. | Hubungan Majapahit dan Sunda pun retak selama berabad-abad. |
| 53 | Historians still debate the details. The story appears in the Kidung Sunda, the Carita Parahyangan and the Pararaton, but not in the Nagarakretagama. | Sejarawan masih memperdebatkan detailnya. Kisah ini tercatat dalam Kidung Sunda, Carita Parahyangan dan Pararaton, tapi Nagarakretagama tidak menyebutnya. |
| 54 | One political ambition turned a celebration of love into a scar on history. | Satu ambisi politik mengubah pesta cinta jadi luka sejarah. |
| 55 | Chapter three. A war inside the house. | Bab tiga. Perang di dalam rumah sendiri. |
| 56 | In 1389, Hayam Wuruk died, and Majapahit split in two. | Tahun 1389, Hayam Wuruk wafat, dan Majapahit terbelah dua. |
| 57 | In the Western Court ruled Wikramawardhana, the king's nephew and son-in-law. | Di Kedaton Barat, berkuasa Wikramawardhana, keponakan sekaligus menantu sang raja. |
| 58 | In the East ruled Bhre Wirabhumi, Hayam Wuruk's son by a concubine, according to the Pararaton. | Di Kedaton Timur, berkuasa Bhre Wirabhumi, yang menurut Pararaton adalah putra Hayam Wuruk dari selir. |
| 59 | Both felt they had the greater right. The quarrel grew, until Bhre Wirabhumi sought support from the Emperor of China. | Keduanya merasa paling berhak. Perselisihan memanas, sampai Bhre Wirabhumi mencari dukungan dari Kaisar Tiongkok. |
| 60 | In 1404, the Paregreg War erupted. | Tahun 1404, meledaklah Perang Paregreg. |
| 61 | Regreg, in Old Javanese, means moving in halts and jerks, and this war indeed lurched back and forth. | Kata regreg dalam bahasa Jawa Kuno berarti bergerak tersendat-sendat, dan memang perang ini berjalan maju mundur. |
| 62 | First the west won, then the east. | Kadang pasukan barat menang, kadang pasukan timur. |
| 63 | Two years of war drained the royal treasury, and left the soldiers exhausted. | Dua tahun perang menguras kas kerajaan, dan membuat para prajurit kelelahan. |
| 64 | In 1406, the western army, led by Bhre Tumapel, broke into the Eastern Court. | Tahun 1406, pasukan barat yang dipimpin Bhre Tumapel, putra Wikramawardhana, menembus Kedaton Timur. |
| 65 | Bhre Wirabhumi fled by boat at night, but was hunted down and killed by Raden Gajah. | Bhre Wirabhumi kabur naik perahu di malam hari, tapi dikejar dan dibunuh oleh Raden Gajah. |
| 66 | The Western Court won. But it was a hollow victory. | Kedaton Barat menang. Tapi ini kemenangan yang semu. |
| 67 | In the chaos, about one hundred and seventy Ming envoys visiting the Eastern Court were killed too. | Di tengah kekacauan, sekitar seratus tujuh puluh utusan Dinasti Ming yang sedang berkunjung ke Kedaton Timur ikut tewas. |
| 68 | Emperor Yongle demanded sixty thousand taels of gold. By 1408, only ten thousand had been paid, and the rest was forgiven. | Kaisar Yongle menuntut ganti rugi enam puluh ribu tahil emas. Sampai tahun 1408, baru sepuluh ribu yang dibayar, sisanya dimaafkan. |
| 69 | Vassal lands began to stop paying tribute, and broke away one by one. | Wilayah-wilayah taklukan mulai berhenti membayar upeti, dan satu per satu melepaskan diri. |
| 70 | The Paregreg War is thought to be one cause of Majapahit's decline. The kingdom finally came to an end around the early sixteenth century. | Perang Paregreg diduga menjadi salah satu penyebab kemunduran Majapahit. Kerajaan itu akhirnya berakhir sekitar awal abad ke-16. |
| 71 | From the fire of Singhasari to a civil war, Majapahit left two lessons. | Dari api Singhasari sampai perang saudara, Majapahit meninggalkan dua pelajaran. |
| 72 | A clever strategy can defeat the biggest army. | Strategi yang cerdas bisa mengalahkan pasukan terbesar. |
| 73 | But no matter how strong a kingdom is, its deadliest enemy often comes from within. | Tapi sekuat apa pun sebuah kerajaan, musuh paling mematikan sering kali datang dari dalam. |
| 74 | Majapahit. 1293 to 1527. | Majapahit, 1293 sampai sekitar 1527. |
