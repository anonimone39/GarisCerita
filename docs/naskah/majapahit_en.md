# Berdirinya Majapahit (1293), 9:16, narasi Inggris

| # | Inggris | Indonesia |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | How does a prince who just lost a war beat the Mongols, one of the most feared armies on Earth, without spending a fortune? | Gimana caranya pangeran yang baru kalah perang bisa mengalahkan Mongol, salah satu pasukan paling ditakuti di dunia, tanpa keluar banyak modal? |
| 2 | This is the story of how Majapahit was born. | Ini kisah lahirnya Majapahit. |
| 3 | In 1292, Jayakatwang of Kediri attacks the kingdom of Singhasari and kills its king. | Tahun 1292, Jayakatwang dari Kediri menyerang Singhasari dan membunuh rajanya. |
| 4 | The king's son-in-law, Raden Wijaya, escapes, then bows to Jayakatwang and pretends to surrender. | Menantu sang raja, Raden Wijaya, berhasil kabur, lalu pura-pura tunduk pada Jayakatwang. |
| 5 | He is given a patch of forest called Tarik, where his men find maja fruit so bitter it makes them gag. | Ia diberi hutan bernama Tarik. Di sana pengikutnya menemukan buah maja yang pahitnya bikin mual. |
| 6 | Quietly, the new village becomes his base, named after that fruit: Majapahit. | Diam-diam, desa baru itu jadi markasnya, dinamai dari buah itu: Majapahit. |
| 7 | Meanwhile, about a thousand ships carrying some twenty thousand Mongol troops arrive on Java. | Sementara itu, sekitar seribu kapal membawa 20.000 pasukan Mongol mendarat di Jawa. |
| 8 | Kublai Khan sent them to punish Singhasari's king, who had insulted his envoy. | Kubilai Khan mengirim mereka untuk menghukum raja Singhasari yang pernah menghina utusannya. |
| 9 | But that king is already dead, and the Mongols have no idea that Jayakatwang rules Java now. | Padahal raja itu sudah tewas, dan Mongol tidak tahu Jayakatwang yang kini berkuasa. |
| 10 | Raden Wijaya sees a golden opportunity. | Raden Wijaya melihat peluang emas. |
| 11 | He sends the Mongol commanders an offer they can't refuse. | Ia mengirim tawaran yang sulit ditolak ke komandan Mongol. |
| 12 | Your enemy is in Kediri, he says. Let's take him down together! | "Musuh kalian ada di Kediri. Ayo kita serang bareng!" |
| 13 | Wijaya's fighters and the Mongol troops march side by side and storm Kediri. | Pasukan Wijaya dan Mongol bergerak bersama menggempur Kediri. |
| 14 | Jayakatwang's army falls apart, and the king himself is captured. | Pasukan Jayakatwang hancur, dan ia sendiri tertangkap. |
| 15 | The Mongols celebrate their easy victory, lay down their weapons, and start to relax. | Mongol berpesta merayakan kemenangan, meletakkan senjata, dan mulai santai. |
| 16 | That's exactly what Wijaya was waiting for. | Inilah yang ditunggu Wijaya. |
| 17 | He asks to return home to prepare gifts for Kublai Khan, then turns on his allies. | Ia pamit pulang untuk menyiapkan upeti bagi Kubilai Khan, lalu berbalik melawan sekutunya. |
| 18 | His forces ambush the Mongol escort and launch a surprise attack on their camp. | Pasukannya menyergap pengawal Mongol dan menyerang mendadak markas mereka. |
| 19 | The Mongols are shocked, they lose thousands of soldiers, and they run for their ships. | Mongol kaget, kehilangan ribuan prajurit, lalu kabur ke kapal. |
| 20 | They sail back to China empty handed, and Kublai Khan never conquers Java. | Mereka pulang ke Tiongkok dengan tangan kosong. Kubilai Khan tak pernah menaklukkan Jawa. |
| 21 | In 1293, Raden Wijaya is crowned the first king of Majapahit. | Tahun 1293, Raden Wijaya dinobatkan sebagai raja pertama Majapahit. |
| 22 | His kingdom would grow into one of the greatest empires in the history of the archipelago. | Kerajaannya kelak jadi salah satu kerajaan terbesar dalam sejarah Nusantara. |
| 23 | Sometimes, a war isn't won by the biggest army, but by the smartest strategy. | Kadang perang tidak dimenangkan pasukan terbesar, tapi strategi paling cerdas. |
| 24 | Want part two, the Battle of Bubat? | Lanjut ke kisah Perang Bubat di part berikutnya? |
