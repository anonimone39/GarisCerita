# Tragedi Perang Bubat (1357), 9:16

| # | Inggris | Indonesia |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | They came to a royal wedding, but never made it home. | Niatnya mau datang ke pesta pernikahan kerajaan, tapi malah pulang tinggal nama. |
| 2 | How did a proposal of love turn into one of the darkest tragedies in the history of the archipelago? | Gimana lamaran cinta bisa berubah jadi salah satu tragedi paling kelam di sejarah Nusantara? |
| 3 | This is the story of the Battle of Bubat. | Ini kisah Perang Bubat. |
| 4 | In 1357, Majapahit is at the peak of its glory under King Hayam Wuruk. | Tahun 1357, Majapahit sedang di puncak kejayaan di bawah Raja Hayam Wuruk. |
| 5 | He falls in love with Dyah Pitaloka, the princess of the Sunda Kingdom, and sends her a proposal. | Ia jatuh cinta pada Dyah Pitaloka, putri Kerajaan Sunda, lalu melamarnya. |
| 6 | King Linggabuana of Sunda agrees, and sets off for Majapahit with his family and a small escort. | Raja Sunda, Prabu Linggabuana, setuju dan berangkat ke Majapahit bersama rombongan kecil. |
| 7 | No war army, just a wedding party carrying gifts. | Bukan membawa pasukan perang, karena niatnya memang mau hajatan. |
| 8 | They camp at Bubat Field, waiting for the wedding day. | Mereka berkemah di Lapangan Bubat, menunggu hari pernikahan. |
| 9 | But trouble comes from Gajah Mada, the prime minister. | Masalah muncul dari Mahapatih Gajah Mada. |
| 10 | He is still obsessed with finishing his Palapa Oath, to unite the whole archipelago under Majapahit. | Ia masih terobsesi menuntaskan Sumpah Palapa, menyatukan Nusantara di bawah Majapahit. |
| 11 | And Sunda is the kingdom that has never bowed. | Dan Sunda adalah kerajaan yang belum juga tunduk. |
| 12 | So Gajah Mada rejects an equal marriage. | Gajah Mada menolak pernikahan yang setara. |
| 13 | He demands that Dyah Pitaloka be handed over as tribute, a sign that Sunda has surrendered. | Ia menuntut Dyah Pitaloka diserahkan sebagai upeti, tanda Sunda takluk pada Majapahit. |
| 14 | Humiliated, King Linggabuana refuses to bow. | Merasa harga dirinya diinjak-injak, Prabu Linggabuana menolak tunduk. |
| 15 | Majapahit troops surround Bubat Field. | Pasukan Majapahit pun mengepung Lapangan Bubat. |
| 16 | The Sundanese are hopelessly outnumbered, with only ceremonial guards. | Rombongan Sunda kalah jumlah telak, hanya membawa pengawal upacara. |
| 17 | Yet they choose to fight to the last drop of blood. | Tapi mereka memilih bertarung sampai titik darah penghabisan. |
| 18 | King Linggabuana falls in battle. | Prabu Linggabuana gugur. |
| 19 | And Dyah Pitaloka chooses to end her own life to protect her honor. | Dan Dyah Pitaloka memilih mengakhiri hidupnya demi menjaga kehormatan. |
| 20 | Hayam Wuruk is too late to stop it. | Hayam Wuruk terlambat menghentikan tragedi itu. |
| 21 | He is heartbroken, and furious. | Ia hancur dan murka. |
| 22 | The bond between Majapahit and Sunda breaks for centuries. | Hubungan Majapahit dan Sunda retak selama berabad-abad. |
| 23 | And Gajah Mada is pushed out of his post. | Sementara Gajah Mada tersingkir dari jabatannya. |
| 24 | One political ambition turned a celebration of love into a scar on history. | Satu ambisi politik mengubah pesta cinta jadi luka sejarah. |
| 25 | So what finally brought down a kingdom as mighty as Majapahit? | Lalu, apa yang akhirnya bikin kerajaan sekuat Majapahit runtuh? |
| 26 | We will cover the civil war in part three: the Paregreg War. | Kita bahas perang saudaranya di Part 3: Perang Paregreg. |
