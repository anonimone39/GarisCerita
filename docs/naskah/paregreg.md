# Perang Paregreg (1404-1406), 9:16

| # | Inggris | Indonesia |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The biggest kingdom in the archipelago was not destroyed by an enemy from outside. | Kerajaan terbesar di Nusantara ini ternyata tidak hancur karena diserang musuh dari luar, |
| 2 | It fell apart over an inheritance fight inside its own house. | tapi runtuh karena rebutan warisan di dalam rumah sendiri. |
| 3 | This is the story of the Paregreg War. | Ini kisah Perang Paregreg. |
| 4 | After King Hayam Wuruk died in 1389, Majapahit split in two. | Setelah Raja Hayam Wuruk wafat tahun 1389, Majapahit terbelah dua. |
| 5 | The main throne in the Western Palace went to Wikramawardhana, the husband of the crown princess. | Takhta utama di Kedaton Barat dipegang Wikramawardhana, suami sang putri mahkota. |
| 6 | In the Eastern Palace ruled Bhre Wirabhumi, the king's son by a concubine. | Di Kedaton Timur, berkuasa Bhre Wirabhumi, putra Hayam Wuruk dari selir. |
| 7 | Each of them believed he deserved the crown more. | Keduanya sama-sama merasa paling berhak berkuasa. |
| 8 | In 1404, the tension exploded into the Paregreg War. | Tahun 1404, ketegangan meledak jadi Perang Paregreg. |
| 9 | The word means a war that comes in stages, back and forth, and wears everyone out. | Artinya perang yang berlangsung bertahap, maju-mundur, dan sangat melelahkan. |
| 10 | At first, the Eastern forces had the upper hand, and pushed deep toward the center of Majapahit. | Awalnya, pasukan Kedaton Timur unggul dan berhasil mendesak pusat Majapahit. |
| 11 | Months turned into years, and the soldiers on both sides grew exhausted. | Bulan berganti tahun, dan prajurit di kedua pihak makin kelelahan. |
| 12 | But Wikramawardhana received fresh troops and turned the tide. | Namun, Wikramawardhana mendapat bantuan pasukan dan membalikkan keadaan. |
| 13 | In 1406, the Eastern Palace was attacked in return. | Tahun 1406, Kedaton Timur diserbu balik. |
| 14 | Bhre Wirabhumi lost, and tried to escape by boat at night. | Bhre Wirabhumi kalah dan mencoba kabur naik perahu di malam hari, |
| 15 | But he was caught and killed. | tapi berhasil ditangkap dan tewas. |
| 16 | The Western Palace won. | Kedaton Barat menang. |
| 17 | But it was a hollow victory. | Tapi ini kemenangan yang semu. |
| 18 | The royal treasury was drained, and the army was shattered. | Kas kerajaan terkuras habis dan pasukan hancur. |
| 19 | Worse, in the chaos, Majapahit troops killed one hundred seventy envoys from Ming China, who were visiting. | Lebih apesnya lagi, di tengah kekacauan, pasukan Majapahit menewaskan 170 utusan Dinasti Ming yang sedang berkunjung. |
| 20 | Ming China demanded a huge fine in gold. | Majapahit pun dijatuhi denda emas dalam jumlah besar. |
| 21 | And one by one, the lands Majapahit ruled across the archipelago began to break away. | Satu per satu wilayah taklukan di Nusantara mulai memisahkan diri. |
| 22 | Since the Paregreg War, the glory of Majapahit never truly recovered, | Sejak Perang Paregreg, kejayaan Majapahit tak pernah benar-benar pulih, |
| 23 | until it finally collapsed around the fifteen hundreds. | hingga akhirnya runtuh sekitar abad ke-16. |
| 24 | The biggest lesson: however strong a kingdom is, its deadliest enemy often comes from within. | Pelajaran terbesarnya: sekuat apa pun sebuah kerajaan, musuh paling mematikan sering kali datang dari dalam. |
